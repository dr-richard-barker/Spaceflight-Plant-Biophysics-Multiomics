LunarLeaf-CFD

A browser-based fluid-dynamics workbench for modelling and visualising the airflow and O2 / CO2 / H2O concentration gradients that form around plant tissue — and how they change as gravity changes (1 g → Mars 0.38 g → Moon 0.17 g → microgravity). A real 2D Lattice-Boltzmann engine with textbook validation gates.

From microgravity boundary-layer biophysics to the spaceflight multi-ome: a coupled lattice-Boltzmann and Farquhar–von Caemmerer–Berry model of Arabidopsis gas exchange and enclosure carbon starvation

Unstirred leaf boundary layers thicken 2.0–2.3× in microgravity, but sealed flight canisters exhaust CO₂ in ~7 minutes, creating a 22× larger starvation effect that cancels symmetrically out of standard flight-vs-ground contrasts.
